Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use KV PropMart website or initiate an inquiry through WhatsApp or phone, you may provide personal information such as your full name, mobile number, email address, property preferences, and budget.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to respond to your property queries, coordinate scheduled site visits, share relevant property brochures/cost sheets, and provide tailored real estate advisory. We do not sell or rent your personal contact information to unrelated third-party marketers.

3. Static Architecture & Data Security

This website operates as a secure static interface without server-side user database storage. When you click WhatsApp inquiry buttons, information is transmitted securely through your own WhatsApp client.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party services such as WhatsApp or mapping services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you access.
